# Store listing copy — Fruit Snake

Ready to paste into App Store Connect and Google Play Console. Tweak to taste,
but keep it honest — misleading listings get rejected.

## App name / title
- **Fruit Snake** (add a subtitle if the name is taken, e.g. *Fruit Snake: Munch & Grow*)

## Subtitle (Apple, 30 chars) / Short description (Google, 80 chars)
- Apple subtitle: `Learn fruit & veggie names!`
- Google short: `A talking snake game that teaches toddlers fruit, vegetable & healthy food names!`

## Full description
```
Fruit Snake is a bright, friendly learning game that teaches your child the
names of fruits, vegetables and healthy snacks — while they play!

Every time the snake collects something, it SAYS THE NAME OUT LOUD in a clear,
cheerful voice and reacts with fun: "Yummy, an apple!" ... "Mmm, green
broccoli!" The word appears in big letters with a sparkle, so children hear it,
see it, and remember it.

Steer your happy snake around the garden, collect healthy food, and grow as long
as you can. The walls are friendly: instead of a harsh "game over," your snake
pops out the other side — so young players stay in the game and keep smiling.

WHAT YOUR CHILD LEARNS
🍎 Fruits — apple, banana, strawberry, grapes, mango and more
🥕 Vegetables — carrot, broccoli, corn, tomato, cucumber and more
🥨 Healthy Snacks — cheese, milk, blueberries, avocado, egg and more
🌈 Rainbow Mix — all 36 words together to review what they know

MADE FOR LITTLE LEARNERS
🗣️ Clear speech — the name is spoken on its own, then a fun sentence
⭐ "NEW!" celebration the first time they discover a word
📊 Progress tracker — see "7 of 12 Fruits learned" fill up as they play
🍓 Colorful boards, googly-eyed snake, sparkles and happy sounds
👶 Big, easy controls — swipe or tap giant arrow buttons
🔇 Voice and sound can be turned off for quiet time
❤️ Kind design: forgiving walls, encouraging words, nothing scary
🎨 Fun snake skins to collect — Grape, Apple, Ocean, Lime, Bubblegum, Golden, Rainbow

Great for toddlers, preschoolers, and early readers. No account needed, and it
works offline.

Fruit Snake is free to play. It contains ads and an optional one-time purchase to
remove ads and unlock all snake skins.
```
