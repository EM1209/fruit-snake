## Subtitle (Apple, 30 chars) / Short description (Google, 80 chars)
- Apple subtitle: `Cute snake — eat fruit, grow!`
- Google short: `A friendly, easy Snake game for kids and families. Eat fruit and grow longer!`

## Full description
```
Fruit Snake is a bright, friendly twist on the classic Snake game — made to be
easy and fun for little kids, and still cheerful for grown-ups.

Steer your happy snake around the garden, munch juicy fruit, and grow as long as
you can. The walls are friendly: instead of a harsh "game over," your snake pops
out the other side — so young players stay in the game and keep smiling.

🍎 Simple one-thumb controls — swipe, tap the big arrow buttons, or use a keyboard
🐍 A cute snake with googly eyes and a flicking tongue
🍓 Colorful fruit and gentle, happy sounds (with a mute button for quiet time)
⭐ Beat your best score
🎨 Fun snake skins to collect — Grape, Apple, Ocean, Lime, Bubblegum, Golden, Rainbow
❤️ Kind design: forgiving walls, encouraging messages, no scary bits

Great for toddlers, preschoolers, and the whole family. No account needed.

Fruit Snake is free to play. It contains ads and an optional one-time purchase to
remove ads and unlock all snake skins.
```
