**[Your Name / Company]** ("we") makes the mobile game **Fruit Snake** ("the
app"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Information we collect
The app itself does **not** ask you to create an account and does **not** collect
your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.

**On-device only:** your best score, sound preference, chosen snake skin, and
whether you purchased "Unlock All" are stored locally on your device. This never
leaves your device except as needed to verify a purchase with the app store.

**Advertising & purchases (third parties):** the app uses:
- **Google AdMob** to show ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage
  data to deliver and measure ads. The app requests **non-personalized ads**
  where required (e.g. for a child-directed audience).
  See <https://policies.google.com/privacy> and
  <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>.
- **The Apple App Store / Google Play (and RevenueCat, if enabled)** to process
  and verify the optional in-app purchase. We do not receive your payment card
  details. See your app store's privacy policy and
  <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>.

## Children's privacy
The app is designed to be family-friendly. Where the app is directed to children,
we configure advertising to request **non-personalized** ads and do not knowingly
collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your choices
- You can remove ads entirely with the one-time "Unlock All" purchase.
- You can reset all local data by deleting and reinstalling the app.
- Device-level ad settings (e.g. "Limit Ad Tracking" / resetting the advertising
  ID) further limit ad personalization.

## Data retention & security
Local data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall. We do not
operate a server that stores your personal data.
