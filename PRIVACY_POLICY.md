**[Your Name / Company]** ("we") makes the mobile game **Fruit Snake** ("the
app"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Information we collect
The app itself does **not** ask you to create an account and does **not** collect
your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.

**On-device only:** your best score, sound preference, chosen snake skin, and
whether you purchased "Unlock All" are stored locally on your device. This never
leaves your device except as needed to verify a purchase with the app store.

**Microphone ("Say it back!") — off unless you turn it on:** the app has an
optional practice mode where, after the game says a word, your child says it
back. It is **off by default** and can only be switched on from behind a
grown-up check (a simple sum). When it is on:

- The microphone is opened **only during a word's turn** — a few seconds after
  each word — and is closed as soon as the turn ends, you skip it, the app is
  backgrounded, or you switch the mode off.
- The app **does not record, store, or transmit any audio**, and keeps no
  transcript. What was heard is compared to the expected word, shown on screen
  for that moment, and discarded.
- Speech is turned into text by **your device's own speech-recognition
  service** (part of the operating system or browser). Some platforms — notably
  Chrome and Android — perform this **in the cloud**, meaning the spoken audio
  is sent to the platform vendor under **their** privacy policy, not ours. We
  never receive it. See <https://policies.google.com/privacy> and
  <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/ask-siri-dictation/>.
- If you would rather no microphone were used at all, choose **"No microphone —
  tap instead"**: the child still says each word out loud, and taps a star when
  they have. Nothing listens. The learning is the same.
- Only the *count* of words your child has said, and which words they were, is
  saved — on your device, as progress.

**Advertising & purchases (third parties):** the app uses:
- **Google AdMob** to show ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage
  data to deliver and measure ads. The app requests **non-personalized ads**
  where required (e.g. for a child-directed audience).
  See <https://policies.google.com/privacy> and
  <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>.
- **The Apple App Store / Google Play (and RevenueCat, if enabled)** to process
  and verify the optional in-app purchase. We do not receive your payment card
  details. See your app store's privacy policy and
  <https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>.

## Children's privacy
The app is designed to be family-friendly. Where the app is directed to children,
we configure advertising to request **non-personalized** ads and do not knowingly
collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

A child's voice can be personal information. That is why "Say it back!" ships
**off**, needs a grown-up to enable it, never records or keeps audio, and offers
a **no-microphone** alternative that teaches the same words. If your family or
jurisdiction treats cloud speech recognition as a step too far, use the
no-microphone option — it is a first-class mode, not a degraded one.

## Your choices
- "Say it back!" can be switched off at any time with the **🎤 Say it** button,
  or set to **no microphone** so nothing listens.
- You can remove ads entirely with the one-time "Unlock All" purchase.
- You can reset all local data by deleting and reinstalling the app.
- Device-level ad settings (e.g. "Limit Ad Tracking" / resetting the advertising
  ID) further limit ad personalization.

## Data retention & security
Local data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall. We do not
operate a server that stores your personal data.
